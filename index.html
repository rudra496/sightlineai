The Solution

Intelligence at Every Step

SightlineAI transforms any camera-equipped device into a real-time accessibility companion — analyzing scenes, reading text, detecting obstacles, and guiding users with AI.

1

📷 Capture & Sense

Camera captures the environment while hardware sensors (LiDAR/IMU) detect obstacles and terrain — all processed in real-time.

2

🧠 AI Analysis

Qwen AI models analyze the scene for objects, text (OCR), hazards, and contextual understanding — with offline fallback for unreliable connectivity.

3

⚖️ Risk Assessment

A dedicated risk engine evaluates danger levels, accessibility scores, and generates prioritized guidance — keeping safety first.

4

🗣️ Intelligent Response

Contextual, multi-language voice and text guidance delivered via WebSocket streaming with conversation memory for natural interaction.
